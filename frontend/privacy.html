Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026 · Demo application
This is a demonstration project. It is not affiliated with American Express and must not be used to process real cardmember data.

What we store

  • Account: email, display name, role, and a salted PBKDF2-SHA256 hash of your password. We never store the password itself.
  • Case data: the messages you send to Sentinel, the decisions the agents reached, and the audit records for those decisions.
  • Feedback: any star rating and comment you submit.

How we use it

Only to run the servicing simulation: to classify your request, propose a compliant action, explain the decision, and keep an audit trail. We do not sell data or share it with third parties.

Logs

Requests are logged as structured JSON for debugging and security. Passwords, tokens, secrets and email addresses are redacted before they are written.

Your rights

  • Export: download everything we hold about you via GET /api/account/export.
  • Deletion: delete your account and case data via POST /api/account/delete. Audit records are retained because they are the immutable compliance trail; they contain a session identifier and the decision, not your credentials.

Data retention & security

Case data is kept until you delete your account. Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 and access is protected by signed, expiring tokens, server-side authorization, rate limiting and security headers.
